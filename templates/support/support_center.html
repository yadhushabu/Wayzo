FAQ

Common questions answered

How are tickets processed?
Once submitted, our support team reviews your complaint and responds within 24–48 hours. You can track status here.
How do I request a refund?
Submit a ticket with your booking ID and reason for refund. Our team will verify and initiate within 3–5 business days.
Can I cancel my package?
Cancellations depend on the package's cancellation policy. Submit a ticket and we'll assist you based on your booking terms.
How do I check ticket history?
All submitted tickets appear in the Support History table on this page with real-time status updates.
